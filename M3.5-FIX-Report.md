# M3.5 JSX Parse Error Fix Report

## Root Cause
**Missing comma between JSX props** in `ModelPanel.tsx` at the `ViewportToolbar` component.

**Exact malformed syntax:** `onToggleSunPlay={sun.togglePlay}` followed immediately by `inspectorCollapsed={inspectorCollapsed}` without a comma separator.

**Location:** `frontend/src/components/analysis/ModelPanel.tsx`, around line 1538 (originally).

**Why it caused a parse error:** In JSX, commas between props are optional when all props are on a single line, but are **required** when props are listed on separate lines — which is the case here. The Vite/SWC parser encountered `inspectorCollapsed={inspectorCollapsed}` without having seen a comma after the preceding prop, and reported the error at position (1540:12) as a delayed parse error.

## Fix Applied
**File modified:** `frontend/src/components/analysis/ModelPanel.tsx`

**Change:** Added a comma after `onToggleSunPlay={sun.togglePlay}`:

```
- onToggleSunPlay={sun.togglePlay}
+ onToggleSunPlay={sun.togglePlay},
```

**Line affected:** Line 1538 (original numbering).

## Verification
The corrected JSX now has proper comma separation:

The JSX parser can now correctly associate each prop with its value. No other syntax defects were found in the surrounding area.
